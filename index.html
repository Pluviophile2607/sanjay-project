03 / Your quick guide

Five questions
before you share.

  1. 01
    Who made it?

    Look for an identifiable publisher, author, or original source.

  2. 02
    When was it made?

    Old stories can look new when they’re reposted without context.

  3. 03
    What’s the evidence?

    Separate a confident claim from links, data, or firsthand reporting.

  4. 04
    How does it make me feel?

    Urgency and outrage are useful signals to slow down.

  5. 05
    Can another source confirm it?

    Cross-check important claims with reliable, independent sources.
